# Mvoon privacy policy

_Effective 2 November 2026_

Mvoon is a video and music player. It is built to work entirely on your phone.

## The short version

- Mvoon **does not collect, store on a server, sell or share any of your data**.
- There is **no account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking and no crash reporting**.
- Everything Mvoon remembers stays **on your device**.

## What stays on your device

To be useful, Mvoon keeps a few things in its own private storage on your phone:

- your library index (file names, folders, durations, cover art thumbnails);
- your playback history and resume positions (for *Continue watching*, *Watching* and *Listening*);
- your favourites, playlists and settings.

None of this leaves your phone through Mvoon. You can erase it at any time with
**Settings → Apps → Mvoon → Storage → Clear data**, or by uninstalling the app.

If you have turned on Android's own device backup, Android may include Mvoon's settings and
history in **your** backup in your Google account. That backup is controlled by you and Google;
the Mvoon developer cannot see it.

## Permissions and why they are needed

| Permission | Why |
|---|---|
| Videos and music on your device (`READ_MEDIA_VIDEO`, `READ_MEDIA_AUDIO`; storage on Android 12 and older) | To find and play the videos and songs on your phone and SD card. You can also allow only selected videos. |
| Internet | Only to play network streams or links **you** open (for example a home media server). Mvoon has no server of its own. |
| Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock | To keep music playing with the screen off and show playback controls in the notification. |

## Children

Mvoon is a general-purpose media player and is not directed at children under 13.
