```
Mvoon is a beautiful video and music player that plays almost anything — and keeps everything private on your phone.

PLAYS ALMOST ANYTHING
• MKV, MP4, AVI, MOV, WebM, TS and more; MP3, FLAC, AAC, Opus, OGG and more
• Dolby Digital (AC-3), Dolby Digital Plus (E-AC-3) and DTS audio built in
• A compatibility engine for older formats like WMV, ASF, RealMedia and DivX
• Hardware decoding for long battery life, with automatic software fallback
• Network streams: HTTP, HLS, DASH and RTSP links

MADE FOR WATCHING
• Pick any audio track a video has: languages, commentary, surround or stereo
• Subtitles found automatically; style them and drag or pinch to move and resize
• Swipe for brightness, volume (boost up to 200%) and seeking; double-tap to skip, double-tap the middle to pause
• Press and hold to watch faster: slide to pick 1.5× up to 4×, let go for normal speed
• Picture-in-picture, resume where you left off, and playback speed
• Audio and subtitle sync controls

MADE FOR LISTENING
• Background playback with lock-screen and headset controls
• Songs, favourites, playlists and recently played
• Sleep timer that gently fades out

A LIBRARY THAT FEELS GOOD
• "What's playing today?": your recently watched videos and recently played songs, one swipe away
• One tap on the play button continues your last video where you left off
• Folders (including SD cards), playlists, and delete what you don't need
• While a video plays: add it to favourites or a playlist, or delete it, without leaving the player
• Light and dark themes, smooth animations, and artwork for files without covers

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• No account, no ads, no analytics, no tracking
• Your library and history never leave your phone

FREE SOFTWARE

```
